Whitepaper

Agentic companies with a human backstop

The problem

Advanced AI can run more and more of a company's daily work. If the value it creates goes to whoever races fastest, safety is traded for speed and the benefits concentrate. The Foundation's answer is structural: put control of the companies where a mission cannot be bought out, and let agents do the operating work inside rules that people set and enforce.

The structure

At the top is the AGI Future Foundation, a California nonprofit public benefit corporation organized for charitable purposes. It holds the mission and, through agreements still to be written, control over the family. AGI Corp is the management company that runs the worlds day to day. W3 LLC is the series company whose 33 named sub-series are the family's operating companies, alongside 6 focus-domain DAOs and 3 program tracks.

How a company runs

Every cell has a management team: its own specialist agents seated in the eight roles the charter gives agents - research synthesis, task routing, proposal drafting, coordination, progress tracking, monitoring, collaborator scouting and grant drafting - with a coordinator holding any role no specialist fits. Agents draft and submit proposals. Only the cell's Steward Council, three human seats deciding two of three with signed votes, can enact, decline, amend the charter or move funds. An agent that tries is held on the record, not obeyed.

The Fiduciary Shield

The shield is the set of protections that let the family put safety ahead of short-term profit: the nonprofit's dedicated purpose, a board that acts in good faith for that purpose, indemnification and insurance, a conflict-of-interest policy, clean separation of books between the nonprofit, the management company and each cell, and written agreements between them. Today 2 of those items are evidenced, 7 are open actions for the board and founder, and 4 are questions for counsel.

Shared benefit

The Foundation describes two further programs: Open Global Investment, broad participation in the cells' economic upside kept separate from control, and a Windfall Clause, a pre-commitment by the cells to share profits above a set threshold. Neither has published terms yet; where the participation interests sit, and the threshold, share and recipient of the windfall, are decisions for the board and counsel.

Where it stands

Built and checked every five minutes: 33 named series worlds (plus ALS DAO and Parkinsons DAO in this index) and 44 management teams with 303 roles seated by the companies' own agents, 154 open proposals waiting on stewards, 600 furnished rooms with 2743 interactive hotspots, 4,300 scaffold spaces. Across the family's two GitHub owners, 55 of 345 repositories are the family's own work: 13 are linked to a company by name, 33 are candidates a steward confirms, and 145 forks are kept as studied references, never as the family's own. Not yet done: the founder-gated publication on RP1's Earth, the management company's and W3 LLC's filings, the stewards' real keys, and every price and payment account.
