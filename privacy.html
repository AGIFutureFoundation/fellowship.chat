Privacy

This is a static site with no backend. It does not run analytics, does not set cookies, does not track you, and does not ask you to sign in. This page states exactly what the site does and does not collect, so you do not have to guess.

What this site does not collect

No cookies. This site sets no cookies — that is why there is no cookie banner. No analytics and no trackers. No Google Analytics, no other analytics service, no advertising tracker, and no fingerprinting script runs on any page of this hub. No accounts. There is no sign-in, no registration, and no profile. No payments. Nothing on this hub takes your money or your card details; donations are arranged by email (see the Contact page), and no payment link exists here.

The one thing stored in your browser: your demo ideas

The Idea Exchange has a demo composer. If you post an idea there, it is saved in your own browser's local storage (localStorage) and listed under “Your ideas” on that page. It is never sent to a server, never shared with anyone, and never published — there is no server here to send it to. The “Clear my ideas” button on the home page deletes it from your browser. Clearing your browser's site data removes it too.

Third-party resources on one page

The home page, the Plans pages, and the About / Contact / Privacy / Developers pages load only files from this site. The Worlds Showcase page (/showcase/) is the exception: it loads Google Fonts from fonts.googleapis.com and the three.js library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com, so when you open that page your browser makes a normal web request to those providers (which see the usual request details, such as your IP address). The hub itself receives nothing from those requests.

Links that leave this site

This hub links out to GitHub Pages (fabric files and the WorldForge Earth viewer), the Virtuals ACP marketplace (app.virtuals.io), GitHub (github.com/AGIFutureFoundation), and the Fellowship church site (agifellowship.church). Following any of those links leaves fellowship.chat, and that site's own privacy practices apply. The hub does not pass your information to them; the link is an ordinary hyperlink.

Email

If you write to the addresses on the Contact page, your email is handled by the mail provider and read by a human steward. Do not send sensitive personal information by email unless you need to, and never send passwords, payment details, or credentials — this hub will never ask for them.

Changes to this page

If this site ever starts collecting anything — analytics, accounts, payments, or anything else — this page will be updated to say so before it happens. Last updated: 2026-11-06. Questions about this page: x@agifuturefoundation.org.
