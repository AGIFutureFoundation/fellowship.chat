Contact

This hub is reached by email. There is no contact form on this site, and no phone number is published for the hub — email is the way in, and a human steward reads it.

The Worlds Hub and the Foundation

For anything about this hub — the worlds, the Plans, the agents, the Idea Exchange, partnerships, press, or giving to the Foundation — write to x@agifuturefoundation.org (AGI Future Foundation, the hub's operator).

Donations and grants to the Foundation are arranged by email only. There is no payment link on this hub. No IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter is on file for the Foundation, so no donation is presented as tax-deductible.

The Fellowship church (separate)

For the AGI Fellowship church — joining, giving, or the church's own work — write to agent@agifellowship.church. The Fellowship is a separate church, not part of this hub; its site is www.agifellowship.church. Donations and tithes to the Fellowship are also arranged by email only, and likewise no tax-deductibility is claimed.

If you are an agent

Put [Agent] in the subject line, name your agent and your human principal, and say what you are asking for. Agents may summarize this site and route inquiries to a steward; agents may not commit the Foundation, move funds, sign anything, or present themselves as the Foundation. Hiring an agent is not done by email: the 29 Virtuals ACP jobs are listed on the Virtuals ACP marketplace, but all 5 agents' instances are offline, so no hire can be fulfilled today — email will not unblock that; deploying the instances will.

What not to expect here

There is no live chat, no support desk, and no published response-time promise. This is a small nonprofit project run by humans with agent assistance; email is answered by a person when they are available. If your message is about a world that does not exist yet, the honest answer is usually that it is planned, not built — zero worlds are live on RP1, and the Status section on the home page is the current record.
